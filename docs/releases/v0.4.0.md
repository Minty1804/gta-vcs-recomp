The first release of this fork of [gothamor/gta-vcs-recomp](https://github.com/gothamor/gta-vcs-recomp): upstream's
native port of **GTA: Vice City Stories** (PS2, SLUS-21590 USA) with this fork's changes on top. Every change to how the
game looks or plays is a setting in the F2 menu, with the PS2's behaviour as one of its choices.

> **No game code or data is in these files.** You need your own **USA (SLUS-21590)** disc as an `.iso`. The launcher
> builds the game on your PC from it (15-60 minutes the first time).

## Installing

**Windows 10 / 11**: download `VCS-Recomp-0.4.0-windows.zip` and extract it into a short local folder (for example
`C:\Games\VCS`: not Program Files, not OneDrive). Start `VCSLauncher.exe` and follow **Folder -> Disc -> Build tools
-> Build -> Play**. Everything the build needs (compiler, Python, libraries) comes with the zip or is downloaded by the
launcher.

**Linux**: `VCS-Recomp-0.4.0-x86_64.AppImage` (make it executable and run it). There is no macOS download.

**Updating** later: the launcher's **Updates** (on Home: **Update now**) follows this fork's releases. Saves
(`%LOCALAPPDATA%\VCSRecomp`) are never touched and are backed up before every change.

Coming from upstream's or another fork's release: extract this one into a new folder; your saves are shared.

## What this fork adds

- **Detailed city distance**: full-detail buildings far out instead of the low-detail city; cars and people drawn
  further (up to 2x), parked cars too.
- **Graphics**: Direct3D 12; HD texture packs in PCSX2's format; fog distance, exposure, sharp trails; ~4,400 distant
  street lights at night; presets for your PC.
- **Mods**: a mod loader like PC GTA's (`modloader/`, switched live in F2); PC Vice City car mods and VC / SA weapon
  mods converted while the game loads.
- **Cutscene characters**: the story characters in gameplay with their cutscene faces and hands, made from your disc
  by `tools/csped.py` (docs/MODS.md).
- **Controls and combat**: the PC games' keyboard scheme; a reload key; free aim from the driver's seat; button prompts
  for your device; headshots that kill.
- **Map and saving**: a GPS route along the roads; quick save anywhere outside a mission.
- **Look**: cutscenes in the PS2's framing; Oswald subtitles; the F2 and F5 menus in the launcher's look.
- **Fixes**: high-frame-rate bugs (taxi tip, sirens, radio name); HUD flicker in the rain; half the draw calls; objects at the top and bottom of the picture (a dish on a
  roof) no longer come and go as the camera tilts.

Every change, with its setting and default: [docs/FORK.md](https://github.com/Minty1804/gta-vcs-recomp/blob/mod-reload-walk-camera/docs/FORK.md).
The work taken from other forks keeps its authors (willzmkv, olaftnt, SkipperSkipTR, ChrisM-Pek, PerikiyoXD,
batinium and others): the README's Credits.
